About MediSwift

Health access, made simple for Cebu City

MediSwift Global Health Inc. exists to make vaccination and essential medical supplies easy to reach — for patients, families, and the nursing & CHN students training to serve them.

Our Mission

To make immunization and essential medical supplies accessible and affordable across Cebu City — meeting patients where they are, and equipping the next generation of nurses with the tools they need to learn and serve.

Our Vision

A Cebu where no student goes without the equipment they need for clinical training, and no family has to travel far for safe, reliable vaccination care.

What Guides Us

Our values

Accessibility

Walk-in and home-service options so care fits your schedule, not the other way around.

Safety & Compliance

DOH-aligned protocols and licensed healthcare staff for every vaccination administered.

Student-Focused

Fair pricing on the stethoscopes, CHN bags, and kits nursing students actually need.

Community-Centered

Proudly based in and serving Cebu City, one barangay visit at a time.
