Vaccination Services

Prevention is the best protection against illness

Vaccines help prevent serious illness before it starts. Every dose is administered by licensed healthcare staff following DOH-aligned protocols — whether you come to Northgate Centre or we come to you.

Available Vaccines

Immunizations for every stage of life

Below are our most requested vaccines. Ask our staff about any vaccine not listed — most adult and child immunizations can be sourced on request.

Adult Vaccines

Hepatitis B

Protects the liver against Hepatitis B infection — recommended for healthcare and nursing students.

Influenza (Flu)

Seasonal protection against influenza, recommended annually for students and frontliners.

Pneumococcal

Helps prevent pneumococcal disease, including certain types of pneumonia.

Tetanus Toxoid (Td/Tdap)

Protects against tetanus and diphtheria — often required for clinical duty and wound care.

Child Immunization

Complete childhood immunization schedule

We follow the DOH National Immunization Program for infants and children, covering routine vaccines from birth through school age. Bring your child's immunization card for an accurate schedule review.

Weight Management

MediSlim Weight Loss Program

A medically-supervised weight management program for adults, featuring Tirzepatide injections administered by licensed healthcare staff.

Tirzepatide Injections

Tirzepatide is a prescription injectable used for chronic weight management. Every MediSlim client starts with a consultation to review medical history and confirm eligibility — dosing and follow-up are managed by our licensed healthcare staff throughout the program.

Book a MediSlim Consultation
MediSlim is available by consultation only. Eligibility, dosing, and pricing are confirmed by our licensed staff based on your medical history.
