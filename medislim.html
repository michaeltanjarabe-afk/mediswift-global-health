MediSlim is MediSwift's weight management program for adults, built around Tirzepatide injections and overseen by licensed healthcare staff from your very first consultation.

The Treatment

Tirzepatide Injections

Tirzepatide is a prescription injectable used for chronic weight management, administered as part of a supervised program — not a walk-in, self-directed treatment.

How the program works

Every MediSlim client starts with a consultation to review medical history and confirm eligibility. From there, dosing and follow-up are managed by our licensed healthcare staff for the duration of the program — nothing is self-administered without medical oversight.
